Oath of Conquest

Tenets

  • Douse the Flame of Hope. Defeat enemies so completely that their will to fight is broken for good.
  • Rule with an Iron Fist. Once you conquer, tolerate no dissent. Your word is law.
  • Strength Above All. You rule until a stronger one arises. Then grow mightier and meet the challenge.

How to Play Him

Open with fear. Use Conquering Presence on a group. Frightened enemies have Disadvantage on attacks and ability checks while they can see you, and can't willingly move closer to you.

Keep it going. Wrathful Smite frightens anyone who resisted. Command "Grovel" knocks a target prone for your allies. At level 7, Aura of Conquest drops frightened foes near you to 0 speed and deals psychic damage each turn.

Save Guided Strike for the hit that matters most, like a Divine Smite on a boss. Watch your bonus action: smites, Lay on Hands and Shield of Faith all compete for it.

Character

Kavrex is the last heir of House Thorundar, a dragonborn clan that once ruled a mountain march before rivals broke it apart. He swore his oath on the ruins of the family keep: order will be restored, by force if necessary, and anyone who preys on the weak will learn what it is to be ruled.

Trait: Speaks little, and never raises his voice. Ideal: Order is mercy; chaos kills more than any tyrant. Bond: He carries his grandmother's iron signet and means to reclaim the seat it belongs to. Flaw: He sees any challenge to his authority as a challenge to fight.

Level Plan

4

Great Weapon Master (Str 17). Once per turn, add your proficiency bonus to heavy weapon damage, plus a bonus-action attack after a crit or kill. Alternatively take +2 Str.

5

Extra Attack, 2nd-level slots, Faithful Steed, Hold Person and Spiritual Weapon. Breath Weapon rises to 2d10 and you gain Draconic Flight.

6

Aura of Protection: you and allies within 10 ft add +3 (your Cha) to saving throws.

7

Aura of Conquest: frightened creatures within 10 ft have speed 0 and take psychic damage equal to half your paladin level when they start their turn there.

8

Ability Score Improvement: +1 Str, +1 Cha (Str 18, Cha 17), or a feat like Sentinel.

9

Abjure Foes adds a second way to frighten with Channel Divinity. Bestow Curse and Fear join your oath spells.

15

Scornful Rebuke: anything that hits you takes psychic damage equal to your Cha modifier.

20

Invincible Conqueror: for 1 minute, resistance to all damage, an extra attack, and crits on 19–20.
